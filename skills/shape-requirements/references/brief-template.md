# Brief template

Synthesize decisions, not the interview transcript. Omit inapplicable sections.
Use the requested format and destination. For a repository brief without another
convention, use `dev/briefs/YYMMDD-short-slug.md`.

```markdown
# <Outcome>

## Goal

Who needs what outcome, and why?

## Requirements and acceptance

The agreed behavior and observable conditions for success.

## Constraints and non-goals

Only material limits and exclusions.

## Open decisions

Only unresolved choices, their consequences, and a recommendation where useful.
```

A clarification gate normally ends inline with a short interpretation and any
material assumptions. Do not create a separate confirmation document or ask
for duplicate approval.
